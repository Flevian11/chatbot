# Accounts and Support

## Account-specific requests
This starter chatbot does not connect to the LWF authentication system, user database, enrolment records, payment records or support ticketing system. It cannot:
- look up a learner's account;
- reset passwords;
- change profile details;
- enrol a learner;
- change grades or assessment attempts;
- issue or revoke certificates;
- inspect private course progress.

## Recommended handling
For account-specific questions, explain that the assistant cannot access private account information. Ask the learner to use the authenticated platform or contact the official LWF support channel.

## Add verified support details here
The LWF administrator should add the official support email, phone number, working hours and escalation process before this knowledge base is used publicly. Do not invent contact information.
