# Assessments and Certificates

## General concepts
An LMS may use quizzes and assignments to assess learning. A certificate may be issued after a learner satisfies configured course completion requirements.

## LWF implementation note
The LWF project has included quiz and certificate functionality in its development work. The precise learner eligibility rules, grading rules, certificate issuance conditions, retake limits and administrative approval processes must be documented from the current deployed application before the assistant gives definitive instructions.

## Safe response rule
Do not tell a learner that they have passed, qualified for, or been issued a certificate. The assistant does not access live learner records in this starter project. Direct account-specific questions to the authenticated LWF platform or support team.
